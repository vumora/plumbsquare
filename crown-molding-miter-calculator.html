Crown Molding & Compound Miter Angle Calculator (38°, 45° & 52° Spring Angles)

Cutting wide crown molding flat on a compound miter saw bed gives you complete stability and longer blade capacity than cutting nested ("upside down and backward") against the fence—provided you set the exact Miter Swivel Angle and Blade Bevel Tilt Angle. This calculator applies exact spherical trigonometry to compute compound saw settings for any wall corner from 30° to 175°.

1. Exact Spherical Trigonometry Formulas for Flat-Cut Crown Molding

Half Corner Angle (C) = Wall_Corner_Angle ÷ 2
Miter Saw Table Angle = arctan( cos(Spring_Angle) ÷ tan(C) ) [for Hopper/Tilted Sides]
Crown Flat Miter Angle = arctan( sin(Spring_Angle) ÷ tan(C) )
Crown Blade Bevel Angle = arcsin( cos(Spring_Angle) × cos(C) )

3. Frequently Asked Questions

What are the miter and bevel angles for 90-degree crown molding with a 38-degree spring angle?

When cutting standard 38-degree spring-angle crown molding flat on the miter saw table for a 90-degree wall corner, set your miter saw table swivel angle to 31.62 degrees (31.6°) and tilt the saw blade bevel to 33.85 degrees (33.9°). That is why most miter saws have permanent detents marked at 31.6° miter and 33.9° bevel!

What are the miter and bevel settings for 45-degree spring-angle crown molding?

For 45-degree spring-angle crown molding on a standard 90-degree inside or outside corner, set the miter saw swivel to 35.26 degrees (35.3°) and the blade bevel tilt to 30.00 degrees (30.0°).

What is crown molding spring angle and how do I measure it?

The spring angle is the angle between the back of the crown molding and the vertical wall when installed against the ceiling. Standard North American colonial crown is either 38° (52/38 profile) or 45° (45/45 profile). Place a framing square against the bottom wall flat and measure the gap against the top ceiling flat to identify yours.

What if my drywall corner is not exactly 90 degrees?

Drywall mud buildup often pushes outside corners to 88°–89° and inside corners to 91°–92°. Measure the true corner angle with a digital angle finder, enter that exact angle into the Wall Corner Angle box above, and cut to the computed tenth of a degree for gap-free joints.

How do I calculate flat miter angles for a 6-sided hexagon or 8-sided octagon picture frame?

For any flat N-sided regular polygon frame (spring angle = 0°), each corner interior angle is ((N - 2) × 180°) ÷ N, and the saw miter cut angle from 0° square is 180° ÷ N (which equals 30.0° for a 6-sided hexagon and 22.5° for an 8-sided octagon).
