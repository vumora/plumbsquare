Wood Fence Calculator – Posts, 2×4 Rails, Pickets & Post-Hole Concrete Bags

Estimating a backyard wood privacy or picket fence requires accurate math for both above-ground lumber and below-ground concrete footings. Unlike basic estimators that guess concrete bags, this Wood Fence Calculator computes the exact cylindrical volume of your auger holes minus the displaced volume of the buried 4×4 or 6×6 wood posts, returning exact bag counts for 80-lb, 60-lb, and 50-lb premix concrete bags.

1. Fence Post, Rail, Picket & Concrete Formulas

Fence Sections (Bays) = ceil( Total_Fence_Length_Ft ÷ Max_Post_Spacing_Ft )
Total Fence Posts = Fence_Sections + 1
Total 2×4 Rails = Fence_Sections × Rails_Per_Section (2 for 4', 3 for 6', 4 for 8')
Total Pickets = ceil( (Total_Fence_Length_Ft × 12) ÷ (Picket_Width_in + Picket_Gap_in) )
Net Concrete per Hole (ft³) = ( (π × (Hole_Dia/2)² × Depth) - (Post_W² × Depth) ) ÷ 1728

3. Frequently Asked Questions

How many bags of concrete do I need per 4x4 or 6x6 fence post?

For a standard 4×4 wood post (actual 3.5" × 3.5") set 24 to 30 inches deep in a 10-inch diameter auger hole, the net concrete volume after subtracting the buried wood post is approximately 0.90 to 1.15 cubic feet—which requires 2 bags of 80-lb concrete (0.60 cu ft/bag) or 2.5 bags of 60-lb concrete (0.45 cu ft/bag) per post.

How deep should fence posts be buried?

The structural rule of thumb is to bury one-third to one-half of the above-ground fence height (plus 6 inches for a tamped gravel drainage base), or extend below the local winter frost line. A 6-foot privacy fence uses 8-foot 4×4 posts buried 24 to 30 inches deep; an 8-foot fence uses 10-foot or 12-foot 6×6 posts buried 36 inches deep.

How do you calculate the number of fence pickets needed?

Convert total fence length to inches and divide by the sum of one picket's actual width plus your desired gap between pickets: Pickets = ceil( (Fence_Length_Ft × 12) ÷ (Picket_Width_in + Gap_in) ). For a tight edge-to-edge privacy fence using 5 1/2 inch dog-ear pickets with zero gap, divide total inches by 5.5.

How many horizontal 2x4 rails are needed per fence section?

Fences up to 4 feet tall use 2 horizontal 2×4 rails per section (top and bottom). Standard 6-foot privacy fences require 3 rails per section to prevent pickets from warping, and 8-foot fences require 4 rails per section.

What diameter hole should I dig for a fence post?

Auger hole diameter should be roughly three times the width of the post: use a 10-inch or 12-inch hole for a 4×4 post, and a 14-inch to 18-inch hole for a 6×6 corner or gate post.
